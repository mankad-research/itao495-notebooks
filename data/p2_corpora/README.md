# Project 2 Corpora

All three corpora are synthetic. Organizations, products, people, and policies are fictional.

| Corpus | Words | Suggested user and use case | Example questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind Outfitters HR Policy Handbook | 2,778 | An employee or new hire asking an HR help desk | How much sick leave can a part-timer carry over? Do holiday and overtime pay stack? What notice must a manager give to resign? |
| Redwood State Applied Analytics Department Handbook | 2,315 | A student or advisor using a department help assistant | When does fall registration open? What happens to my assistantship if I go on probation? Can I use AI on my capstone? |
| BrewMate 300 Product Manual | 1,977 | A customer using a product-support chatbot | How often must I descale on the default setting? What does E7 mean? Can I program a small batch? |

## Suggested workflow
1. Choose a corpus (or the options below).
2. Write your 20-question test set: 10 direct, 5 that need two passages, 5 the documents cannot answer.
3. Use `Example_Test_Set_HR_Handbook.csv` as a model of the format.

## Other options
- The Apple 10-K from Notebook 18 (Session 18) is a fourth option.
- You may propose a public document set (for example, a city code, a university catalog, or a product manual) for instructor approval before you begin. It should run roughly 2,000 to 10,000 words and be free to use.
