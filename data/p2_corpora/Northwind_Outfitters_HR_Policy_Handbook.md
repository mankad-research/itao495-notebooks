# Northwind Outfitters HR Policy Handbook

*Version 4.2, effective January 1, 2026. Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company.*

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose and Scope
This handbook sets out the employment policies of Northwind Outfitters, a retailer of outdoor gear headquartered in Boise, Idaho. It applies to everyone at our 84 stores, our Nampa distribution center, and the Boise corporate office. Version 4.2 takes effect on January 1, 2026 and replaces the 2024 edition. Where the law gives an employee more than this handbook does, the law applies.

### 1.2 People Operations and Terminology
The human resources department at Northwind is called People Operations. Its service desk is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Mountain Time (peopleops@northwind-outfitters.example). In this handbook the words "employee," "team member," and "associate" mean the same thing.

## 2. Employment Categories

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
A full-time employee is regularly scheduled for 32 or more hours per week. Full-time employees are eligible for the paid time off, holiday, parental leave, and benefits described here, subject to the waiting periods in each section.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees
A part-time employee is regularly scheduled for fewer than 32 hours per week. Part-time employees receive a reduced set of benefits, described in Section 12. If a part-time employee works more than 32 hours in each of 8 consecutive weeks, the store manager must ask People Operations to review the employee for reclassification as full-time.

### 2.3 Seasonal Employees
A seasonal employee is hired for a fixed period of no more than 120 days, usually between October 15 and January 15 to cover the holiday selling season. Seasonal employees are not eligible for paid time off, holiday pay, parental leave, or benefits, but they do earn sick leave. A seasonal employee who accepts a part-time or full-time position within 14 days after the season ends keeps the seasonal days toward the introductory period.

### 2.4 Introductory Period
Every new full-time and part-time employee has a 90-day introductory period. During this period the employee may not use paid time off and is not eligible for the floating holiday. The manager completes a 90-day review when the period ends.

## 3. Working Hours and Overtime

### 3.1 Workweek and Scheduling
The Northwind workweek runs from Sunday at 12:00 a.m. through Saturday at 11:59 p.m. The standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week. Store schedules are posted at least 14 days in advance.

### 3.2 Breaks
An employee working a shift longer than 6 hours receives an unpaid 30-minute meal break. Every employee also receives a paid 10-minute rest break for each 4 hours worked.

### 3.3 Overtime
Hourly (non-exempt) employees are paid 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek, and double time for hours worked beyond 12 in a single day. Overtime must be approved in advance by the shift supervisor. Salaried (exempt) employees, including store managers and all corporate directors, are not eligible for overtime pay. Hours paid as paid time off, sick leave, or holiday pay do not count as hours worked when overtime is calculated.

## 4. Paid Time Off and Sick Leave

### 4.1 Accrual Rates
Paid time off (PTO, also called vacation time) and sick leave accrue as shown below. Northwind pays employees every other week, which makes 26 pay periods per year.

| Category | Years of service | PTO accrual | Yearly PTO | Sick leave accrual | Yearly sick cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time | Under 3 years | 3.08 hours per pay period | 80 hours | 1 hour per 30 hours worked | 48 hours |
| Full-time | 3 to under 7 years | 4.62 hours per pay period | 120 hours | 1 hour per 30 hours worked | 48 hours |
| Full-time | 7 years or more | 6.15 hours per pay period | 160 hours | 1 hour per 30 hours worked | 48 hours |
| Part-time | Any | 1 hour per 40 hours worked | 52 hours | 1 hour per 40 hours worked | 32 hours |
| Seasonal | Any | None | 0 hours | 1 hour per 40 hours worked | 16 hours |

### 4.2 Requesting and Using PTO
A new employee cannot use PTO until the 90-day introductory period ends. A request for 5 or more consecutive workdays needs 30 days' notice; a shorter request needs 7 days' notice. Stores do not approve PTO for November 20 through December 24, the holiday blackout period.

### 4.3 Carryover
On December 31, up to 40 unused PTO hours carry over to the new year, and any hours above 40 are forfeited. Employees with 7 or more years of service may carry over up to 60 hours.

### 4.4 Sick Leave
Sick leave (sometimes called sick time) may be used for the employee's own illness or injury, for medical appointments, or to care for a family member. The employee must tell the supervisor at least 1 hour before the start of the shift. An absence of 3 or more consecutive scheduled shifts requires a note from a health care provider, delivered to People Operations. Unused sick leave carries over from year to year up to the yearly sick cap for the employee's category. Unused sick leave is never paid out.

## 5. Parental Leave

### 5.1 Eligibility and Length
A full-time employee with 12 months of continuous service receives 12 weeks of paid parental leave at 100 percent of base pay after the birth of a child, if the employee is the birth parent. A non-birth parent, adoptive parent, or foster parent receives 6 weeks at 100 percent of base pay. A part-time employee with 12 months of service who averaged at least 20 hours per week receives the same number of weeks, paid at 50 percent of base pay. Seasonal employees are not eligible.

### 5.2 Notice and Timing
An employee should give People Operations 30 days' written notice when the leave can be foreseen. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be taken in one block or in two blocks, each lasting at least 2 weeks.

### 5.3 Leave, PTO, and Benefits
Parental leave runs at the same time as any leave required by law. The PTO blackout period does not apply to parental leave. PTO and sick leave continue to accrue while an employee is on paid parental leave. Benefits continue on the same terms as before the leave began.

## 6. Holidays

### 6.1 Company Holidays
Northwind observes six company holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Stores are closed only on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. A full-time employee receives 8 hours of holiday pay for any company holiday that he or she does not work. Part-time employees are paid only for hours worked.

### 6.2 Holiday Premium
An hourly employee who works on a company holiday is paid 1.5 times the regular rate for the hours worked that day. The holiday premium and the overtime premium do not add together; for each hour, the higher rate applies.

### 6.3 Floating Holiday
Each full-time employee who has finished the introductory period receives one floating holiday of 8 hours per calendar year. It must be used by December 31, is not carried over, and is not paid out. Part-time employees are not eligible. The floating holiday is separate from PTO. When a company holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed on the Friday before, and when it falls on a Sunday it is observed on the Monday after.

## 7. Remote and Hybrid Work

### 7.1 Who May Work Remotely
Only corporate office roles in Boise are eligible. Store roles and distribution center roles are not eligible. A hybrid employee must work at the office at least 3 days per week, and Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are core days when every hybrid employee is expected on site. A fully remote arrangement needs written approval from the vice president of the employee's department.

### 7.2 Eligibility Conditions
To work remotely, an employee must have finished the introductory period and must hold a most recent performance rating of Meets Expectations or higher. An employee placed on an improvement plan loses remote and hybrid eligibility until the plan ends.

### 7.3 Equipment and Stipends
Hybrid employees receive an internet stipend of $40 per month, and fully remote employees receive $60 per month. Each remote or hybrid employee may claim a one-time home office allowance of $300 through the Expense Portal. A single equipment purchase above $150 needs the manager's approval before it is made. Remote employees must use the company VPN.

### 7.4 Working From Another State
An employee may work up to 10 days per calendar year from a state other than Idaho without approval. Longer stays need approval from People Operations because of tax rules.

## 8. Expense Reimbursement and Travel

### 8.1 Submitting Expenses
Expenses are submitted through the Expense Portal within 30 days of the purchase. A receipt is required for any expense above $25. Reimbursement is paid within 10 business days after approval. An expense submitted more than 30 days late needs director approval, and an expense submitted more than 90 days late is not reimbursed.

### 8.2 Approval Limits
A manager may approve a single expense of up to $500. A director approves expenses from $501 to $2,500. A vice president approves anything above $2,500.

### 8.3 Travel Limits
Air travel is booked in economy class, except that a flight longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with director approval. The hotel limit is $180 per night, or $250 per night in New York City, San Francisco, Boston, and Washington, D.C. The meal allowance is $65 per day: $15 for breakfast, $20 for lunch, and $30 for dinner. Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile. Alcohol is never reimbursed. The only exception to the daily meal allowance is a client dinner, which may cost up to $75 per person if the manager approves it in advance. Corporate cards are issued to directors, vice presidents, and any employee who takes more than 8 business trips per year.

## 9. Code of Conduct

### 9.1 Standards
Every team member is expected to act honestly, respect others, and follow safety rules.

### 9.2 Conflicts of Interest and Gifts
An employee must disclose a possible conflict of interest to the Ethics Office within 10 business days of learning of it. An employee may accept gifts from a vendor worth up to $50 per vendor per calendar year. Anything above that amount must be reported to the Ethics Office.

### 9.3 Attendance
Three no-call/no-show shifts within any rolling 12-month period lead to a termination review. Calling in sick at least 1 hour before a shift is not a no-call/no-show.

### 9.4 Confidentiality and Social Media
Employees must not share pricing or unreleased products outside the company. Only the Marketing department may post on behalf of Northwind.

## 10. Harassment and Discrimination Reporting

### 10.1 Policy
Northwind has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.

### 10.2 How to Report
An employee can report a concern in any of three ways. First, the employee can tell any manager, who must forward the report to the Employee Relations Office within 1 business day. Second, the employee can contact the Employee Relations Office directly at employeerelations@northwind-outfitters.example. Third, the employee can call the Ethics Line at 1-800-555-0142, which is run by an outside company, is open 24 hours a day, and accepts anonymous reports.

### 10.3 Investigation and Retaliation
The Employee Relations Office acknowledges each report within 2 business days and aims to finish its investigation within 30 calendar days. Reports should be made within 180 days of the incident, but later reports are still accepted. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation and can lead to discipline up to termination.

## 11. Performance Reviews

### 11.1 Review Cycle
The Northwind fiscal year runs from February 1 through January 31. Annual reviews are completed by March 15, and a mid-year check-in takes place in September. New employees receive a 90-day review. Seasonal employees receive only an end-of-season evaluation.

### 11.2 Ratings
Reviews use four ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets Expectations, and Does Not Meet Expectations.

### 11.3 Merit Increases
Merit increases take effect on April 1 for employees rated Meets Expectations or higher who were hired before October 1 of the fiscal year being reviewed. The target increase is 4 percent for Exceeds Expectations and 2.5 percent for Meets Expectations.

### 11.4 Improvement Plans
An employee rated Does Not Meet Expectations is placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan. While on the plan, the employee is not eligible for a merit increase or for a transfer to another role.

## 12. Benefits Eligibility and Enrollment

### 12.1 Who Is Eligible
Full-time employees are eligible for medical, dental, vision, basic life insurance, and the retirement savings plan, beginning on the first day of the month after 60 days of employment. Part-time employees are eligible only for dental, vision, and the retirement savings plan, beginning on the first day of the month after 90 days of employment. Seasonal employees are not eligible for benefits.

### 12.2 Enrollment Windows
A newly eligible employee has 31 days from the eligibility date to enroll; an employee who misses the window must wait for open enrollment. Open enrollment runs from November 1 through November 15 each year, and new elections take effect on January 1. After a qualifying life event, such as marriage or the birth of a child, the employee has 30 days from the event to change elections.

### 12.3 Cost Sharing
Northwind pays 80 percent of the monthly premium for employee-only medical coverage and 60 percent of the premium for dependent coverage. These shares do not change while an employee is on paid parental leave.

## 13. Termination and Notice

### 13.1 At-Will Employment
Employment at Northwind is at will: either the employee or the company may end it at any time, subject to the law.

### 13.2 Resignation Notice
Hourly employees and store employees are asked to give 2 weeks' notice of resignation. Salaried managers and corporate employees are asked to give 4 weeks' notice. An employee in good standing who gives the full notice is eligible for rehire.

### 13.3 Layoffs and Severance
Northwind gives 60 calendar days' written notice of a layoff that affects 50 or more employees at one site. A full-time employee whose position is eliminated receives severance of 1 week of base pay per year of service, with a minimum of 2 weeks and a maximum of 12 weeks. Part-time and seasonal employees are not eligible for severance.

### 13.4 Termination for Cause
Gross misconduct, such as theft, violence, or harassment, and three no-call/no-show shifts in a rolling 12-month period can lead to termination for cause. An employee terminated for cause is not eligible for severance or rehire.

### 13.5 Final Pay and Property
The final paycheck is issued within 5 business days after the last day of work and includes payment for accrued, unused PTO. Unused sick leave is not paid. The employee must return badges, keys, and company laptops within 3 business days. Benefits end on the last day of the month in which employment ends.

## 14. Use of Company AI Tools

### 14.1 Approved Tools
The Information Security team keeps the list of approved AI tools. The approved tools are Northwind Assist, a chat assistant, and ShelfSense, an inventory forecasting tool. Employees may not use other AI tools for Northwind work.

### 14.2 Data Rules
Customer personal data, such as names, addresses, and payment card numbers, must never be entered into any AI tool, approved or not. Confidential vendor pricing and unreleased product designs may be entered only into Northwind Assist.

### 14.3 Training and Review
An employee who receives access to an approved AI tool must complete the 45-minute Responsible AI Use training within 30 days, and repeat it every year. Any AI-generated text sent to a customer must be reviewed by a person first. AI tools may not be used to make or recommend hiring, termination, or performance rating decisions. Prompts and outputs in approved tools are logged and kept for 12 months.

### 14.4 Reporting Violations
Suspected exposure of data through an AI tool must be reported to Information Security within 24 hours. Violations are handled under the Code of Conduct in Section 9 and may lead to discipline up to termination.
