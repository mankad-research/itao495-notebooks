# BrewMate 300 Programmable Coffee Maker: User Manual

*Model BM-300. BrewMate is a fictional brand. Please read this manual before first use and keep it for reference.*

## 1. Safety Warnings

### 1.1 Important Safeguards
- Plug the coffee maker only into a grounded 120 V, 60 Hz outlet. Do not share the outlet with another high-power appliance.
- Do not use an extension cord. The supplied power cord is 36 inches (91 cm) long.
- Leave at least 4 inches (10 cm) of free space behind and beside the unit, and at least 10 inches (25 cm) above it.
- Never operate the unit without water in the reservoir.
- Never immerse the base, the cord, or the plug in water or any other liquid.
- Use only the BrewMate carafe, model BM-C10, with this unit.
- This appliance is intended for household use only. Children under 8 years old must not use it, and older children need adult supervision.

### 1.2 Hot Surfaces and Steam
The warming plate can reach 176 °F (80 °C). Allow 30 minutes for the unit to cool before cleaning or moving it. Keep your face away from the basket holder when opening the lid during or just after brewing, because hot steam escapes.

### 1.3 Damage and Repairs
Stop using the coffee maker if the cord, plug, or carafe is damaged, and contact BrewMate Customer Care. Do not attempt to repair the unit yourself.

## 3. First-Use Setup

### 3.1 Unpack and Wash
Remove all packaging and labels. Wash the carafe, lid, filter basket, and basket holder by hand in warm, soapy water, and rinse them well.

### 3.2 Install the Filter Cartridge
Soak one cartridge in cold water for 15 minutes, then rinse it under running water for 30 seconds. Place it in the cartridge holder inside the reservoir.

### 3.3 Set the Clock
Plug in the unit. Press CLOCK, then use HR and MIN to set the time. The display uses a 12-hour clock, and a dot appears next to the time for PM.

### 3.4 Set the Water Hardness
The hardness setting tells the coffee maker how often to remind you to descale. Hold STRENGTH and MIN together for 3 seconds. The display shows H1, H2, or H3, and pressing MIN cycles through them. H1 is soft water (up to 60 ppm), H2 is medium water (61 to 120 ppm), and H3 is hard water (above 120 ppm). The factory default is H2. A test strip is included in the box.

### 3.5 Rinse Cycles
Fill the reservoir to the 10-cup mark with fresh water and run two full brew cycles with no coffee in the basket. Pour the water out after each cycle.

## 4. Brewing

### 4.1 Filling the Reservoir
Pour water into the reservoir up to the fill mark for the number of cups you want. The carafe holds 10 cups, where one cup is 4 fl oz (120 ml). The top fill mark is at 1.25 liters.

### 4.2 Adding Coffee
Use medium-ground coffee. A full carafe needs 7 level scoops (70 g). For smaller batches, use about 1 scoop for every 1.5 cups, rounded up. The permanent basket does not need a paper filter, but a No. 4 cone paper filter may be used for a cleaner cup.

### 4.3 Strength
Press STRENGTH to choose Regular or Bold. Regular is the default and brews 10 cups in about 9 minutes. Bold slows the flow of water over the grounds and takes about 12 minutes for 10 cups. The water temperature at the showerhead is 195 to 205 °F (90 to 96 °C).

### 4.4 Small Batch Mode
Press SMALL BATCH when brewing 2 to 4 cups. This mode adjusts the flow for a smaller amount of coffee. The unit does not brew fewer than 2 cups.

### 4.5 Keep Warm and Pause-and-Serve
After brewing, the warming plate stays on for 2 hours and then switches off automatically. Press and hold KEEP WARM to choose 1 hour, 2 hours, or 4 hours. You may remove the carafe during brewing to pour a cup, but you must put it back within 30 seconds or the basket may overflow.

## 5. Programming the Timer

### 5.1 Setting a Brew Time
Set the clock first. Press PROG, then use HR and MIN to enter the start time, and press PROG again to confirm. Press AUTO so that the AUTO indicator lights. The unit then brews at the programmed time every day until you press AUTO again to switch it off. Fill the reservoir and add coffee the night before.

### 5.2 What a Programmed Brew Uses
A programmed brew uses the last strength you selected. Small Batch mode is not available for programmed brews, so a programmed brew always makes a full carafe. Make sure the carafe is in place before you go to bed.

### 5.3 Power Interruptions
If power is lost, the unit remembers the clock and the program for 15 minutes. After a longer interruption the display shows E5, and you must set the clock and the program again.

### 5.4 Timer Lockout
If the DESCALE light has been on for more than 14 days, the timer is disabled and the display shows E7. Descale the unit to restore the timer.

## 6. Descaling Schedule

### 6.1 How Often to Descale
Mineral scale builds up inside the heater and tubing. How often you must descale depends on the hardness setting you chose in Section 3.4. At H1, descale every 120 days. At H2, descale every 90 days. At H3, descale every 60 days. The DESCALE light comes on when descaling is due.

### 6.2 Descaling Procedure
Use BrewMate descaling solution, product code DS-30. Do not use vinegar, which can damage the seals. Remove the filter cartridge and the coffee. Dissolve one 30 g sachet in 1 liter of water and pour it into the reservoir. Hold BREW for 5 seconds. The unit pumps half of the solution through, pauses for 30 minutes, and then pumps the rest. Afterward, run two full reservoirs of fresh water through the unit to rinse it. The whole process takes about 75 minutes.

### 6.3 After Descaling
The DESCALE light turns off by itself when the cycle is complete, and the timer works again. Keep a note of the date, because BrewMate Customer Care may ask for your descaling record when you make a warranty claim.

## 7. Cleaning

### 7.1 After Every Brew
Throw away the grounds and rinse the basket and the carafe.

### 7.2 Weekly Cleaning
The carafe and the permanent filter basket may be washed on the top rack of a dishwasher. The carafe lid, the basket holder, and the reservoir must be washed by hand. Wipe the showerhead and the outside of the unit with a damp cloth once a week, after the unit has cooled. Never use abrasive pads or scouring powder.

### 7.3 Filter Cartridge
Replace the charcoal filter cartridge every 60 days. The two cartridges in the box therefore last about 4 months.

## 8. Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Reservoir empty or below the minimum level | Refill the reservoir and press BREW |
| E2 | Carafe missing or not seated | Place the carafe correctly under the basket holder |
| E3 | Overheating | Unplug the unit and let it cool for 30 minutes |
| E4 | Reservoir not seated | Remove and reseat the reservoir |
| E5 | Clock and program lost after a power interruption | Set the clock and program again |
| E6 | Heater fault | Unplug the unit and call Customer Care |
| E7 | Descaling overdue; timer disabled | Descale the unit as described in Section 6 |
| E8 | Water flow blocked | Check the basket for clogging, then descale |
| E9 | Button stuck | Unplug the unit and press each button to free it |
| E10 | Basket holder open | Close the basket holder fully |

## 9. Troubleshooting

### 9.1 Coffee Is Weak
Check that you used 7 scoops for a full carafe and a medium grind. Try the Bold setting. If the unit has not been descaled on schedule, descale it.

### 9.2 Brewing Takes Too Long
A full carafe on Regular should take about 9 minutes. A longer time usually means scale buildup (lime deposits). Check the hardness setting and descale the unit.

### 9.3 Leaks Around the Basket
Do not fill the basket beyond 7 scoops, and make sure the grind is not too fine. Check that the basket holder is closed.

### 9.4 Timer Did Not Start
Make sure the AUTO light is on and that the clock shows the correct AM or PM. Look for code E5 or E7.

### 9.5 Warming Plate Is Cold
The hot plate switches off 2 hours after brewing unless you chose a longer setting.

### 9.6 Display Is Blank
Check that the plug is fully inserted and that the outlet works. If the display stays blank, call Customer Care before returning the unit to the store.
